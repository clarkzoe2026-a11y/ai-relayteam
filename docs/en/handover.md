# Changing hands

If a worker is unavailable, the lead retries at most once and reports why it failed. The lead may do the work itself only after the user confirms, and may not use a paid call to get around a quota limit.

If the lead is unavailable, a message the user sends directly in another AI's interface without `[LEAD DISPATCH]` on its first line makes that AI the acting lead. It first reads the three status files, reports known facts, boundaries, and Next, then waits for user authorization. At the top of `HANDOFF.md`, it records an "Acting Lead" section with takeover time, scope, changes, and items not yet checked. When the original lead returns, it reviews the interim changes before updating status. Start by marking another AI's own account AGENT_REPORTED.
